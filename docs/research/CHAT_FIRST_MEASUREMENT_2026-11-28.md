# Chat-first measurement: incremental release

A/B demo forms are optional history/training, not prerequisites. Existing ability and habit records, calibrated-research gates, consent, and raw score APIs are retained.

## Habit
The independent ordinal chat indicator uses at most 12 distinct dated completed opportunities and reports the middle category (or middle two categories for an even count). Only owner-scoped, opted-in conversation annotations with exact grounded initiation evidence enter it; unknown and unlinked records do not. Missing messages are not failures. After 45 days the display is explicitly historical. The formal visual progress policy and review scheduling remain unchanged. The 0–4 chat labels are provisional, not the validated SRBAI or probability of habit formation. Neither model confidence nor repetition count alone establishes automaticity.

## Capability
Everyday actions and outcomes are the default evidence view; they are not latent ability scores. The assistant may ask one neutral, relevant question to elicit independent reasoning, evidence, and actual outcomes. Do not infer ability from message count, word length, task delegation, self-confidence or fluency. Proposed next step: dimension-specific anchored performance rubrics, independently double-rated examples, held-out users and tasks, inter-rater agreement and uncertainty, cross-language and assistance checks. Only promote a new scoring policy after improvement over a simple baseline without increased false-positive evidence or burden. No population norms, causal growth claims or fabricated scores.
